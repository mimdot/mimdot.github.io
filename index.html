Mapping magnetic fields
in the space between stars.

I am an independent researcher in astrophysics, mapping magnetic fields in the interstellar medium with radio polarimetry and Faraday tomography.

Email me ORCID GitHub

Research

Faraday tomography of the Perseus molecular cloud

Tracing magnetic fields in and around a star-forming cloud through Faraday rotation, with the GMIMS international team. Supervised by Dr. Mehrnoosh Tahani, University of South Carolina.

Radio-IR correlation in IC 342

Where my research began: separating thermal and non-thermal radio emission in the nearby galaxy IC 342 to probe how cosmic rays propagate. Published in Astronomy & Astrophysics.

HII regions in M33, contributing scientist

Contributing to a team study of radio continuum emission and SED fitting of HII regions in the Triangulum Galaxy.
